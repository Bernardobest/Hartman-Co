Who we are

Hartman & Co LLC is a legal-technology company headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico, operating as part of the Specter & Co Holdings group. We build software, not legal advice: our systems read government notices, regulatory assessments, and formal correspondence, and produce the written response each one calls for.

  • Government fines & penalties
  • Tax & contribution assessments
  • Formal demand & collection notices
  • Administrative appeals & correspondence

Hartman & Co is a software provider. We do not provide legal representation or legal advice; outputs are informational documents prepared for the client's own use.

What we do

Every document carries a rule, a deadline, and a set of facts. Our engine reads all three, matches them against the applicable provisions, and drafts the response — citing the specific rule it relied on, so nothing is a black box.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Upload the notice, fine, or letter exactly as received.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    The system identifies the applicable rule, the deadline, and the available grounds for response.

  3. 03

    Respond

    A complete, ready-to-send response is generated, citing the relevant provisions.

  4. 04

    Track

    Deadlines and delivery are tracked through to resolution — by certified mail with proof of receipt, where the jurisdiction requires it.

Frequently asked

Is Hartman & Co a law firm?

No. We are a software company. We don't provide legal representation, and nothing we produce is legal advice — our systems generate informational documents based on the rules that apply to your situation. For matters requiring representation, we recommend consulting a licensed attorney.

What happens to my documents after I submit them?

Your document is processed to extract what's needed to prepare a response, then retained only as long as necessary to deliver that response and support any follow-up you request. We do not sell or share your documents with third parties.

How fast is the turnaround?

Most documents are analyzed and a response drafted within minutes of submission. Certified mail delivery, where applicable, follows postal timelines beyond our control.

Which markets do you currently serve?

We're established in the European market, with operations centered in France. We're extending the same engine to the United States, starting in New Mexico.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the type and volume of documents you need handled. Get in touch below and we'll walk you through it.
