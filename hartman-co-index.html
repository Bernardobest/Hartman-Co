Who we are

Hartman & Co LLC is a legal-technology company headquartered in Albuquerque, New Mexico, operating as part of the Specter & Co Holdings group. We build software, not legal advice: our systems read government notices, regulatory assessments, and formal correspondence, and produce the written response each one calls for.

  • Government fines & penalties
  • Tax & contribution assessments
  • Formal demand & collection notices
  • Administrative appeals & correspondence

Hartman & Co is a software provider. We do not provide legal representation or legal advice; outputs are informational documents prepared for the client's own use.
