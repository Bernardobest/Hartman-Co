02 — How it works

From notice to response, automatically

Every notice carries a rule, a deadline, and a set of facts. Our engine reads all three, matches them against the applicable provisions, and drafts the response — citing exactly what it relied on, so nothing is a black box.

03 — What it reads

Five categories, one engine

Government and institutional mail comes in a narrow number of shapes, however differently each agency dresses it up. Our engine is built around five of them — the categories that account for most of what crosses a desk, whether that desk belongs to an individual, a business, or the firm representing either.

Traffic & regulatory fines

An automated, camera-issued infraction and one written up by an officer in person run on different clocks and accept different defenses. The engine tells them apart before it drafts anything.

Tax & assessment notices

It lines the notice up against the filing and the records behind it, line by line, to find exactly where the two disagree — and what that disagreement is worth.

Contribution & benefit decisions

A demand for payment and a decision about a benefit follow entirely different appeal bodies and clocks. The engine routes each one to the right track.

Collection & demand letters

Before recommending that anything be paid, it checks whether the sender actually holds the power to enforce what they're asking for, and tests the underlying debt against the rules that can time it out.

Formal regulatory correspondence

Beyond those four, agencies issue a wide range of formal notices, appeals, and procedural letters. The same discipline — read the rule, meet the deadline, cite the basis — applies across all of them.

Coverage varies by jurisdiction as we expand market by market — see Markets below for where we currently operate.

06 — Questions

Frequently asked

Is Hartman & Co a law firm?

No. We are a software company. We don't provide legal representation, and nothing we produce is legal advice — our systems generate informational documents based on the rules that apply to your situation. For matters requiring representation, we recommend consulting a licensed attorney.

What happens to my documents after I submit them?

Your document is processed to extract what's needed to prepare a response, then retained only as long as necessary to deliver that response and support any follow-up you request. We do not sell or share your documents with third parties.

How fast is the turnaround?

Most documents are analyzed and a response drafted within minutes of submission. Certified mail delivery, where applicable, follows postal timelines beyond our control.

Can a law firm offer this directly to its own clients?

Yes. Under a partnership arrangement, firms can put the engine in front of their own clients as an intake step, with review and sign-off staying with the firm.

Which markets do you currently serve?

We're established in the European market, with operations centered in France. We're extending the same engine to the United States, starting in New Mexico.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the type and volume of documents you need handled. Get in touch below and we'll walk you through it.
